PSU Vanguard

UI/UX, Web Design, Branding

Throughout my time as the Web Designer at PSU Student Media Tech, my main project has been redesigning the website of our school's newspaper, the Vanguard.

The site in its current state has needed an update to its website for quite a while now. It was built back in 2015 from a Wordpress template, so it looks like a blog from that era. Along with appearing outdated, it was designed to be evergreen, causing a lot of little issues with the UX to develop over time.

The Vanguard wanted a site that was classic and timeless, playing off the newspaper's deep roots in Portland. My focus throughout was on a clean, minimalist design inspired by the sites professional newspapers that also have a rich history, as well as making sure that every aspect was evergreen and modular so that the site could last longer than a decade without needing a redesign.

AugTag

UI/UX, App Prototype, Augmented Reality

Check out the Figma prototype!

AugTag is an app prototype that resolves the challenge of providing users a means to share something. It uses Augmented Reality to allow users a way to virtually tag their surroundings without having to deal with the negative stigma around graffiti and street art.

I was already interested in using this project to try an AR-related concept, and decided that I could tie it into my heritage as a Portlander, given this city’s robust art culture. I took inspiration from the aesthetic typically referred to as “Grind Fiction” and “Shibuya Punk” for its bold, fun style and love for urbanism and street art to infuse AugTag with energy and love for sharing art with those around us.

Figma Endtag

Motion Design, Branding

For this project, I was tasked with selecting the logo of a company of my choosing and creating a short animation tag of it that could be used in promotional material. It was expected to be roughly five seconds long. This was a really interesting challenge, given that I generally tend to overshoot the runway with the ideas that I have.

Given how much I enjoy using Figma, I decided to created an endtag using their logo, focusing on their value of collaboration in the design process, following their tagline, “Nothing Great is Done Alone.” to show a story of users working together to create the Figma logo and tagline in a Figma file.

Sunrise Project

Branding, Web Design, Illustration

Check out the Figma prototype!

The Sunrise Project is a branding campaign built around promoting solar power that is fun and exciting, with a variety of deliverables.

The goal of the campaign is to use fun, bright colors, expressive typography, a playful tone, and merchandise to bring attention to the benefits of using solar power and renewable energy and encourage users to spend time researching further into the subject.

Spella Caffè

Web Design, Branding, Web Development

Check out the site!

This project is a conceptual redesign of a local company's website using HTML and CSS, with a focus on making the site responsive to both desktop and mobile.

I chose a local coffee roaster due to how well-defined their brand is along with the points of improvement that I could make. I pushed the minimalism that Spella strives for, focusing on high-quality content that makes them excellent.

Coin Boy

UI/UX, App Prototype

Check out the Figma prototype!

Coin Boy is an app prototype built around the idea of enchanting an everyday object. We were tasked with selecting a random item that we found at home and bringing it to class, then determining how we could design an interface that would improve the way that a user interacts with it.

I had gone to an arcade earlier that week and still had the playcard from it, so I built a arcade app that is as convenient as a playcard while also creating the interactive experience that coins have. The app also seeks to gamify the arcade experience with experience points and tracking high scores.
