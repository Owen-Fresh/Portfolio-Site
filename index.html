Hello!

I am Owen Cook, a Graphic Design student at Portland State University.

My areas of interest in the fields are UI/UX, Motion Design, and Branding. I am most fascinated with how we as users interact with technology and interfaces. I am also a chronic worrier, which I channel as an interest in the future and what we have to look forward to in the next decade or so. The future of design and our society as a whole is at an incredibly unique precipice right now, and I am both thrilled and terrified to be alive to witness it.

Thanks for coming by, and good luck, have fun!
